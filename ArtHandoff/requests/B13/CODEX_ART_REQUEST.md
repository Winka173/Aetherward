## 3. Batch notes
Tiers and tint rules: ART_BIBLE §7 'VFX tiers' and COMMON_CONTRACT §4. Fields and zones are top-down circular ground decals (the game squashes them to 0.62), soft edges, inner alpha <= 55% so units stay readable on top; their identity comes from the field's element and name (table below). Impacts: small, fast-reading, <= 60% alpha over the centre. Projectiles point +X. `spark` is WHITE only (tinted). `coin` is a small ancient gold coin with a rune.
