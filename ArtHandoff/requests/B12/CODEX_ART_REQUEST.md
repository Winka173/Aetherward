## 3. Batch notes
Follow the B01 battlefield method exactly (Blender scene placed from the biome's own arena geometry, ground depth = y / 0.62, ortho 38.3 deg, 30 units across 2400 px; 8 layers per ART_BIBLE §7; painterly finish that never moves lanes or nodes). Each guide in references/guides/ shows THAT biome's lanes, nodes, special-node traits, hazard points and covers - hazard and cover spots must read as places where something happens (vents, rift scars, lightning-rod battlements, flooded stairs, ice walls) without glowing like decals. Biome identity (from data): molten_crater = basalt bridges over lava, vents beside them; frostfall_bastion = fortress in a blizzard, frozen tiles, ice covers, diagonal gates; shattered_aether = floating islands over the rift; stormspire_citadel = citadel on a storm peak, battlements, lightning rods; sunken_archive = drowned library, flooded north/south stairs, tome nodes. Keep the ground value budget (15-45%) - the Crystal stays the focal point.

Crystal path overlays (new, 320x400, same pivot as the Crystal, transparent except the addition, registered to the B01 crystal_intact): `crystal_path_bastion` = a carved stone-and-gold bastion collar and a faint shield shell; `crystal_path_pulse` = an arcane ring-halo of runes around the upper crystal; `crystal_path_thorn` = earth-thorn spikes growing from the collar. Overlays are drawn ON TOP of any crystal state.
